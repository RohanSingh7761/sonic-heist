What happens in a heist?

Find who is in earshot

Your phone pings the room and everyone on patrol answers. You never see what they hold.

Sneak to their seat

Their phone signs a ticket. You take 5 to 25% of their stake and post a bond of 20% of the loot.

10 seconds to vanish

Then they find out. Stay out of range for 60 seconds and the loot is yours.

Or get hunted

Your victim pulses a radar and closes in. If they catch you, they win your bond.

What if someone robs you?

Your phone sounds the alarm. You get one minute to walk them down.

How do you install it?

Android checks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Check what you need first.

  1. Download

    Tap Download on your phone. If Chrome warns about APK files, tap Download anyway.

  2. Allow this source

    Open the file. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then go back.

  3. Install and play

    Tap Install, open Sonic Heist and allow the microphone.
