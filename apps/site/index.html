How does a round go?

See who is around

Your phone listens for friends nearby. Everyone playing pops up on your radar. Pick your mark.

Sneak up close

Creep in until your phones can hear each other, then swipe a few of their coins. It’s a dare: get caught and you hand it all back, plus a little extra.

10 seconds to vanish

Then their phone goes off. Stay out of reach for a minute and the coins are yours.

Or get caught

Your friend follows the radar and closes in. If they find you, they win your coins instead.

What if a friend robs you?

Your phone sounds the alarm. You get one minute to look around the room and find who did it.

Who do you play as?

Pick a character. It follows you down this page, and your friends will see it on their radar.

Every clean getaway and every catch levels your character up.

What do you need?

An Android phone
iPhone is coming. Until then, ask the team for an Expo Go invite.
Your microphone
It only decodes game signals. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.
Friends in the room
That’s the whole point. Phones find each other by sound, so it only works when you are together: a party, the office, a long train ride.
No real money
Coins are free play money. Nothing you can buy, nothing you can lose.

How do you install it?

Android checks before installing apps from outside the Play Store. Check what you need first.

  1. Download

    Tap Download on your phone. If Chrome warns about APK files, tap Download anyway.

  2. Allow this source

    Open the file. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then go back.

  3. Install and play

    Tap Install, open steal-eth, allow the microphone and grab your friends.
